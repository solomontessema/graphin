Graphin is a web app for collecting and connecting notes, web pages and other sources. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Graphin at graphin.org and the Graphin Save browser extension, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

Who runs Graphin

Graphin is run by Solomon Tessema. For any question about this policy or your data, contact solomon.tessema@ionnova.com.

What we collect

Your account. Your email address (which is your username), your first and last name, and a password. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, never as typed.

Your content. The networks, tiles, links, notes, labels, tags and relationships you create, and the positions and styles you give them.

Pages you save with the extension. When you save a page, a link or selected text, the extension sends the page address, its title, the text you selected, and the addresses of its preview image and icon. It sends nothing else from the page, and only when you click Save or use the right-click menu.

The extension on your computer. It keeps the Graphin address it uses, the network you last saved to, and the list of your networks, so the right-click menu can show them. This stays in your browser and is removed when you remove the extension.

Technical information. A sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in (it is needed for the service to work), and the standard records a web server keeps, such as the time of a request.

Why we use it

  • To run Graphin: to store and show your networks, and to let you save items to them.
  • To keep your account secure: to send the email that confirms your address, and the link that resets a forgotten password.
  • To keep the service working and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

Graphin is hosted by IONOS, and email is sent through the same provider. These are the only services that handle your data on our behalf. When you open a link in Graphin, the site it points to, such as a video or web page, loads in your browser and follows its own privacy policy.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on servers in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and content for as long as your account exists. Links that expire, such as those for confirming an address or resetting a password, are kept only until they expire or are used.

Your choices

  • You can change or delete any network, tile, link or note at any time.
  • You can delete your account by contacting us at solomon.tessema@ionnova.com. We remove your account and content.
  • You can remove the Graphin Save extension at any time, which removes what it stored.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Security

The site uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. The sign-in cookie is marked so that scripts on other sites cannot read it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Children

Graphin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
